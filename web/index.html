How to review

For each case, look at the enhanced photograph (B) and compare it with the original (A). Panel C shows where vessels changed; panel D zooms into the yellow boxes: original · enhanced · enhanced with outlines · red-free original.

Click No if the enhanced image has invented, missing, displaced or distorted vessels; otherwise click Yes (passes). The next unanswered case opens automatically. You can return to any case from the list on the left and change the answer.

Keys: Y yes, passes · N no, hallucination · ← → previous / next · Enter enlarge the image.
