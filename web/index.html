Working through the cases

Choose Yes or No (or press Y / N); the next case opens by itself. You can go back to any case from the list on the left and change it. On the photo, scroll to zoom in, drag to move around, and double-click (or Reset view) to see it whole again; Full size opens it on the whole screen.

Keys: Y yes · N no · ← → previous / next · Enter full size · 0 reset view.

The eye at the top left switches the page between bright and dark; the line under it says which one it switches to.

Your annotations are kept in this browser on this computer only; nothing is uploaded. You can close the page and continue later in the same browser. When finished, click Download annotations and send us the file. To continue on another computer, download the annotations here, then use Load annotations there.
