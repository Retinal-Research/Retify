Welcome to Retify

A computer made each poor photo of the eye clearer. Sometimes it gets the vessels wrong. Your task: say whether the clearer picture still shows the same vessels.

  1. Compare the enhanced picture (B) with the original (A).
  2. In panel C: blue = new vessel, red = lost vessel. The colors are only a guide; trust your eyes.
  3. Click Yes if the vessels are the same, No if vessels were added, lost, moved or bent. Or press Y / N.

Scroll on the picture to zoom in. How to at the top shows this again.

How to review

Each picture has four parts: A the original, B the enhanced picture, C the vessels of both (blue = new vessel, red = lost vessel, green = in both), D close-ups of the yellow boxes.

Click No if vessels in B were added, lost, moved or bent compared with A; otherwise click Yes. The next case opens by itself. You can go back to any case from the list on the left and change your answer.

On the picture: scroll to zoom in, drag to move around, double-click (or Reset view) to see it whole again. Full size opens it on the whole screen.

Keys: Y yes, passes · N no, hallucination · ← → previous / next · Enter full size · 0 reset view.
